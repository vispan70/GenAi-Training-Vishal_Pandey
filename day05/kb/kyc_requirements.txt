VISHAL PANDEY BANK
Know Your Customer (KYC) Requirements

Document Type: Customer Compliance Guide
Bank: Vishal Pandey Bank
Version: 1.0
Effective Date: 2026-01-01

OVERVIEW

Know Your Customer (KYC) is the customer identification and verification process used by Vishal Pandey Bank before providing certain banking services.

KYC helps the bank verify customer identity and maintain accurate customer records.

BASIC KYC INFORMATION

Customers may be required to provide:

- Full legal name
- Date of birth
- Residential address
- Mobile number
- Email address
- Identity information
- Tax identification information where applicable
- Photograph where required

ACCEPTABLE DOCUMENTS

Depending on the applicable process, customers may be asked to provide officially accepted identity and address documents.

Documents must:
- Be valid
- Be readable
- Match the customer's information
- Satisfy the applicable verification requirements

PAN / TAX INFORMATION

Customers may be required to provide PAN or another applicable tax identifier for relevant banking services and transactions.

DIGITAL KYC

Where supported, Vishal Pandey Bank may offer digital KYC or video-based verification.

Customers must follow the instructions provided through the official banking channel.

Customers should not share KYC documents with unknown individuals or unofficial websites.

ADDRESS VERIFICATION

If the customer's current address differs from the address shown in existing records, additional address verification may be required.

KYC UPDATES

Customers should keep their KYC information up to date.

A KYC update may be required when:
- Personal information changes
- Address changes
- Identification documents expire or require replacement
- The bank requests periodic verification
- Regulatory requirements change

NAME CHANGE

If a customer's legal name changes, supporting documentation may be required before bank records can be updated.

MOBILE NUMBER UPDATE

Customers should update their registered mobile number through an authorized Vishal Pandey Bank process.

A verification procedure may be required.

KYC FAILURE

KYC may not be completed if:
- Documents are unclear
- Information is inconsistent
- Required documents are missing
- Verification cannot be completed
- Additional information is required

CUSTOMER RESPONSIBILITY

Customers are responsible for providing accurate and current information.

Customers should immediately report suspected misuse of their identity documents.

SECURITY WARNING

Vishal Pandey Bank will not ask customers to disclose confidential authentication credentials such as:
- ATM PIN
- UPI PIN
- OTP
- CVV
- Internet banking password

This document is fictional and intended for the Vishal Pandey Bank RAG training project. Actual KYC requirements depend on applicable laws and regulatory requirements.
