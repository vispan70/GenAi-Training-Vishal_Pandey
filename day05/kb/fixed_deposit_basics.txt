Fixed Deposit Basics

Document Type: Product Guide
Bank: Vishal Pandey Bank
Version: 1.0
Effective Date: 2026-01-01

OVERVIEW

A Fixed Deposit (FD) allows a customer to deposit a specified amount with Vishal Pandey Bank for a selected tenure at an applicable interest rate.

The deposited amount is generally held until maturity unless the customer requests premature closure according to applicable terms.

KEY FD TERMS

Principal:
The amount initially deposited into the fixed deposit.

Tenure:
The period for which the deposit is maintained.

Interest Rate:
The applicable annualized rate offered for the selected FD product and tenure.

Maturity Date:
The date on which the FD reaches the end of its selected tenure.

Maturity Amount:
The principal plus applicable interest, subject to the selected FD type and applicable terms.

ELIGIBILITY

Eligible individuals and other permitted customer categories may open an FD subject to Vishal Pandey Bank's account-opening requirements.

MINIMUM DEPOSIT

The minimum amount required to open an FD depends on the selected FD product.

TENURE

Customers can select from available FD tenures offered by the bank.

Interest rates can differ between tenures.

INTEREST CALCULATION

Interest is calculated according to the applicable Vishal Pandey Bank FD product rules.

The final maturity amount depends on:
- Principal
- Tenure
- Applicable interest rate
- Interest calculation frequency
- Selected payout option
- Applicable deductions or taxes

INTEREST PAYOUT OPTIONS

Depending on the product, customers may select:

1. Cumulative FD

Interest is accumulated and paid along with the principal at maturity.

2. Non-Cumulative FD

Interest is paid periodically according to the selected payout frequency.

PREMATURE WITHDRAWAL

Customers may request premature closure of an FD.

Premature closure may result in:
- Reduced interest
- Applicable penalty
- Different interest calculation
- Other applicable charges

The exact impact depends on the FD product terms.

FD RENEWAL

Customers may choose automatic renewal if supported by the selected FD product.

At maturity, the deposit may:
- Renew automatically
- Transfer principal and interest to the linked account
- Follow another maturity instruction selected by the customer

FD RECEIPT

Customers should retain the FD confirmation/receipt details for their records.

LOAN AGAINST FD

Eligible customers may be able to obtain a loan or overdraft against an FD, subject to product eligibility and applicable terms.

TAXATION

Interest earned on an FD may have tax implications. Applicable tax deductions and reporting depend on prevailing regulations and the customer's circumstances.

IMPORTANT

FD interest rates are subject to change. The rate applicable to a new FD depends on the rate available at the time the deposit is created.
