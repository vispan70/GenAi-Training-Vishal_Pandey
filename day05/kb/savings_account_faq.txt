VISHAL PANDEY BANK
Savings Account - Frequently Asked Questions

Document Type: Customer FAQ
Bank: Vishal Pandey Bank
Version: 1.0
Effective Date: 2026-01-01

OVERVIEW

A Vishal Pandey Bank Savings Account is designed for customers who want a secure account for deposits, withdrawals, digital payments, and everyday banking.

ACCOUNT OPENING

Q1. Who can open a savings account?

Any eligible individual who satisfies Vishal Pandey Bank's account-opening and KYC requirements can open a savings account.

Q2. What documents are required?

Customers generally need:
- Valid identity proof
- Valid address proof
- PAN or applicable tax identification
- Recent photograph, where required
- Mobile number and email address

Additional documents may be requested depending on the customer's profile and regulatory requirements.

Q3. Can I open an account online?

Yes. Vishal Pandey Bank may provide digital account-opening facilities. Customers must complete the required identity verification and KYC process before the account becomes fully operational.

MINIMUM BALANCE

Q4. Is there a minimum balance requirement?

Minimum balance requirements depend on the savings account variant. Some account variants may have zero-balance requirements, while others may require a specified average monthly balance.

Customers should check the applicable product terms before opening an account.

Q5. What happens if I do not maintain the required balance?

If the selected account has a minimum-balance requirement, applicable charges may be imposed according to the current Vishal Pandey Bank Charges Schedule.

INTEREST

Q6. Does a savings account earn interest?

Yes. Eligible savings accounts earn interest according to the applicable interest rate and calculation methodology published by Vishal Pandey Bank.

Q7. When is savings-account interest credited?

Interest is calculated according to the bank's applicable policy and credited to eligible accounts at the prescribed frequency.

ATM AND DEBIT CARD

Q8. Can I receive a debit card with my savings account?

Yes. Eligible savings-account customers can request or receive a Vishal Pandey Bank debit card subject to applicable terms.

Q9. What should I do if my debit card is lost?

Immediately hotlist or block the card using the available Vishal Pandey Bank channels, such as mobile banking, net banking, customer support, or an eligible branch.

DIGITAL BANKING

Q10. Can I use UPI with my savings account?

Eligible customers can link their savings account to supported UPI services after completing the required registration and verification.

Q11. Can I use net banking?

Yes. Eligible customers can activate Vishal Pandey Bank Net Banking and use supported services such as balance enquiry, fund transfers, statement downloads, and bill payments.

WITHDRAWALS AND TRANSFERS

Customers can withdraw funds through supported ATMs and branches and can transfer funds using supported digital banking channels.

Transactions may be subject to daily limits, security checks, and applicable charges.

ACCOUNT CLOSURE

To close a savings account, the customer must submit a closure request through an approved Vishal Pandey Bank channel. Any outstanding dues, holds, or pending transactions must be resolved before closure.

CUSTOMER SUPPORT

For account-related assistance, customers should contact Vishal Pandey Bank customer support or visit an authorized branch.
