Credit Card Types and Features

Document Type: Product Information
Bank: Vishal Pandey Bank
Version: 1.0
Effective Date: 2026-01-01

OVERVIEW

Vishal Pandey Bank offers different fictional credit card variants designed for different spending requirements.

1. VISHAL PANDEY BANK CLASSIC CREDIT CARD

The Classic Credit Card is designed for customers looking for a basic credit card for everyday purchases.

Typical features:
- Domestic transactions
- Online purchases
- Contactless payments
- ATM cash withdrawal subject to applicable terms
- Monthly statements
- Reward eligibility according to the card program

Best suited for:
Customers who primarily need a general-purpose credit card.

2. VISHAL PANDEY BANK REWARDS CREDIT CARD

The Rewards Credit Card is designed for customers who frequently use their card for eligible purchases.

Typical features:
- Reward points on eligible transactions
- Online transaction support
- Contactless payments
- Monthly statements
- Reward redemption through supported channels

Reward points may vary by transaction category and merchant type.

3. VISHAL PANDEY BANK TRAVEL CREDIT CARD

The Travel Credit Card is designed for customers who frequently travel.

Typical features:
- Travel-related reward benefits
- International transaction support
- Contactless payments
- Travel offers where applicable
- Reward redemption according to program rules

Foreign currency transactions may be subject to applicable foreign exchange conversion charges.

4. VISHAL PANDEY BANK CASHBACK CREDIT CARD

The Cashback Credit Card is designed for customers who prefer cashback benefits instead of traditional reward points.

Eligible transactions may earn cashback according to the applicable cashback program.

Cashback may have:
- Category restrictions
- Monthly limits
- Minimum redemption requirements
- Exclusions for certain transaction types

5. VISHAL PANDEY BANK PREMIUM CREDIT CARD

The Premium Credit Card is designed for customers seeking enhanced benefits and higher-value rewards.

Possible features include:
- Enhanced reward earning
- Travel-related benefits
- Premium customer support
- Selected merchant offers
- Higher credit limits subject to eligibility

Eligibility depends on the customer's credit profile, income, internal bank policy, and other applicable criteria.

CREDIT LIMIT

The credit limit is assigned by Vishal Pandey Bank after evaluating the customer's eligibility and risk profile.

The credit limit is not automatically equal to the customer's income.

CASH WITHDRAWAL

Credit-card cash withdrawals may be permitted subject to:
- Available cash limit
- Applicable fees
- Applicable interest
- Security restrictions

Customers should review applicable charges before making cash withdrawals.

BILL PAYMENT

Customers should pay at least the minimum amount due by the payment due date.

Paying only the minimum amount may result in interest charges on the remaining outstanding balance.

CARD SECURITY

Customers should never share:
- PIN
- CVV
- OTP
- Card password
- Net banking password

Vishal Pandey Bank will never require customers to disclose confidential authentication credentials through unsolicited calls or messages.

IMPORTANT NOTE

All products and features described here are fictional and created for the Vishal Pandey Bank RAG training project.
