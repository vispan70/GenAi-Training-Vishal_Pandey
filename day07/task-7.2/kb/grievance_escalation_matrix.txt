VISHAL PANDEY BANK
Customer Grievance and Escalation Matrix

Document Type: Customer Support Procedure
Bank: Vishal Pandey Bank
Version: 1.0
Effective Date: 2026-01-01

PURPOSE

The Vishal Pandey Bank grievance process provides customers with a structured method for reporting unresolved banking issues.

Customers should first attempt to resolve the issue through the bank's standard customer-support channels.

LEVEL 1 - CUSTOMER SUPPORT

The first level of escalation is Vishal Pandey Bank Customer Support.

Customers can report:
- Transaction failures
- Account-service issues
- Debit-card problems
- UPI issues
- Net Banking issues
- General service complaints

Customers should provide:
- Customer/account reference where appropriate
- Transaction reference number
- Date and time
- Amount involved
- Description of the issue
- Relevant supporting information

A complaint/reference number should be retained for future follow-up.

LEVEL 2 - BRANCH OR SERVICE MANAGER

If the issue is not resolved through standard customer support, the customer may escalate the complaint to the relevant Vishal Pandey Bank branch or service manager.

The customer should provide the original complaint/reference number.

LEVEL 3 - GRIEVANCE REDRESSAL OFFICER

If the complaint remains unresolved after the applicable first-level process, it can be escalated to the designated Vishal Pandey Bank Grievance Redressal Officer.

The escalation should include:
- Original complaint number
- Previous communication
- Details of the unresolved issue
- Expected resolution
- Supporting documents, if relevant

LEVEL 4 - PRINCIPAL NODAL / SENIOR GRIEVANCE AUTHORITY

For complaints that remain unresolved after the previous stages, the customer may escalate the matter to the bank's designated senior grievance authority according to the applicable grievance policy.

The escalation should reference all previous complaint numbers.

ESCALATION MATRIX

Level 1:
Customer Support
Purpose: Initial complaint registration and basic resolution.

Level 2:
Branch/Service Manager
Purpose: Review unresolved service issues.

Level 3:
Grievance Redressal Officer
Purpose: Formal grievance review.

Level 4:
Senior/Principal Grievance Authority
Purpose: Final internal escalation within the bank.

COMPLAINT INFORMATION

A well-documented complaint should include:

1. Customer name
2. Registered contact details
3. Account/service involved
4. Transaction reference, if applicable
5. Date and time of incident
6. Amount involved, if applicable
7. Description of the issue
8. Previous complaint/reference number
9. Requested resolution
10. Supporting documents

UPI COMPLAINTS

For UPI complaints, customers should include:
- UPI transaction reference
- Transaction date
- Amount
- Recipient details where applicable
- Transaction status
- Whether the account was debited

UNAUTHORIZED TRANSACTIONS

For suspected unauthorized transactions:

1. Immediately secure or block the affected card/account channel.
2. Report the transaction through an official Vishal Pandey Bank channel.
3. Obtain a complaint/reference number.
4. Provide supporting details.
5. Monitor subsequent transactions.

IMPORTANT SECURITY WARNING

Customers should never include confidential authentication information in a complaint.

Do not provide:
- UPI PIN
- ATM PIN
- CVV
- OTP
- Net Banking password
- Mobile Banking password

The bank does not require these credentials to register a normal service complaint.

ESCALATION BEST PRACTICE

Customers should retain:
- Complaint numbers
- Emails or written communication
- Transaction references
- Dates of communication
- Responses received from the bank

This information helps the bank investigate unresolved complaints efficiently.
