OVERVIEW

This document provides fictional indicative charges for selected Vishal Pandey Bank services.

Charges can vary depending on the customer's product, account type, transaction type, channel, and applicable terms.

SAVINGS ACCOUNT

Minimum-balance non-maintenance:
Charges may apply to account variants that have a minimum-balance requirement.

Account statement:
Digital statements may be provided at no charge. Physical or special statement requests may attract applicable charges.

DEBIT CARD

Debit card replacement:
A replacement fee may apply for lost, stolen, damaged, or expired cards depending on the card variant.

ATM TRANSACTIONS

ATM transactions may be subject to applicable transaction limits and charges.

Charges can depend on:
- Vishal Pandey Bank ATM usage
- Other-bank ATM usage
- Transaction type
- Number of transactions
- Account variant

FUND TRANSFER

Certain fund-transfer services may have applicable charges depending on:
- Transfer method
- Transaction type
- Customer account type

UPI

Standard eligible UPI transactions are generally intended to be available through supported channels without a customer-facing transaction fee.

However, specific services or exceptional transaction types may have separate charges according to applicable terms.

CREDIT CARD

Credit-card charges may include:
- Annual fee
- Joining fee
- Late-payment fee
- Cash withdrawal fee
- Foreign currency transaction fee
- Interest on outstanding balances
- Over-limit charges where applicable

The applicable fee depends on the specific credit-card variant.

PERSONAL LOAN

Loan-related charges may include:
- Processing fee
- Documentation charges
- Late-payment charges
- Prepayment/foreclosure charges where applicable
- Other applicable service charges

FIXED DEPOSIT

Premature closure of an FD may result in a reduced interest rate or applicable penalty according to the selected product terms.

NET BANKING

Standard digital account services may be offered without a separate service charge, while selected transactions or special requests may attract charges.

CHARGE DISCLOSURE

Customers should review the applicable charges before using a paid service.

The charge applicable to a transaction is determined by the product and tariff applicable at the time the transaction is processed.

IMPORTANT

This document intentionally uses general and fictional charges rather than claiming to represent actual Vishal Pandey Bank fees.
