VISHAL PANDEY BANK
Personal Loan Eligibility

Document Type: Loan Product Guide
Bank: Vishal Pandey Bank
Version: 1.0
Effective Date: 2026-01-01

OVERVIEW

A personal loan is an unsecured loan that eligible customers may use for permitted personal financial requirements.

Because the loan is unsecured, collateral is generally not required. Approval is subject to the bank's credit and risk assessment.

BASIC ELIGIBILITY

A customer may be considered for a personal loan based on:

- Age
- Income
- Employment status
- Employment stability
- Existing financial obligations
- Credit history
- Credit score
- Banking relationship
- Repayment capacity
- Internal risk assessment

AGE

The applicant must satisfy Vishal Pandey Bank's applicable minimum and maximum age criteria at the time of application and/or maturity.

INCOME

Applicants must demonstrate sufficient and verifiable income.

Income documents may include:
- Salary slips
- Bank statements
- Income tax documents
- Employment information
- Other income evidence requested by the bank

EMPLOYMENT

Salaried applicants may need to demonstrate stable employment.

Self-employed applicants may need to provide business and income documentation.

CREDIT HISTORY

The bank may review the applicant's credit history and repayment behavior.

A strong repayment history can improve the applicant's credit profile, but approval is never guaranteed solely based on credit score.

EXISTING LOANS

Existing loan and credit-card obligations are considered when evaluating repayment capacity.

A customer with substantial existing obligations may receive a lower eligible amount or may not qualify.

LOAN AMOUNT

The approved loan amount depends on the applicant's:
- Income
- Repayment capacity
- Credit profile
- Existing obligations
- Employment/business stability
- Bank risk assessment

INTEREST RATE

The applicable interest rate depends on the loan product and customer risk assessment.

Interest rates may vary between applicants.

DOCUMENTS

Applicants may be asked for:
- Identity proof
- Address proof
- PAN/tax identification
- Income proof
- Bank statements
- Employment/business documents
- Additional documents required for verification

APPLICATION PROCESS

Step 1:
Submit a personal-loan application.

Step 2:
Provide required identity and income documents.

Step 3:
Complete verification.

Step 4:
The bank evaluates eligibility and creditworthiness.

Step 5:
If approved, the customer receives the applicable loan terms.

Step 6:
After acceptance and completion of required formalities, the loan may be disbursed.

REASONS FOR POSSIBLE REJECTION

A personal-loan application may be rejected because of:
- Insufficient income
- High existing debt
- Poor credit history
- Incomplete documentation
- Unstable employment
- Inability to verify information
- Internal risk criteria

IMPORTANT

Meeting basic eligibility criteria does not guarantee loan approval.
