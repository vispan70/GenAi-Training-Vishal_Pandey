VISHAL PANDEY BANK
Net Banking Activation and Login Guide

Document Type: Digital Banking Guide
Bank: Vishal Pandey Bank
Version: 1.0
Effective Date: 2026-01-01

OVERVIEW

Vishal Pandey Bank Net Banking allows eligible customers to access supported banking services through an internet-enabled device.

AVAILABLE SERVICES

Depending on account eligibility, customers may access:
- Account balance
- Account statements
- Fund transfers
- Beneficiary management
- Bill payments
- Debit card services
- FD services
- Profile management
- Service requests

ELIGIBILITY

Customers with an eligible Vishal Pandey Bank account and registered contact information can request Net Banking activation.

ACTIVATION PROCESS

Step 1:
Visit the official Vishal Pandey Bank Net Banking login or registration page.

Step 2:
Select the registration or first-time user option.

Step 3:
Enter the requested customer/account information.

Step 4:
Complete identity verification using the registered authentication method.

Step 5:
Create the required login credentials according to the bank's password policy.

Step 6:
Complete registration.

Step 7:
Log in using the newly created credentials.

PASSWORD REQUIREMENTS

Customers should create a strong password.

A strong password should:
- Be difficult to guess
- Not contain easily identifiable personal information
- Not be reused across multiple services
- Be kept confidential

LOGIN SECURITY

Customers should:
- Use only the official Vishal Pandey Bank website
- Avoid banking through public or unsecured computers
- Log out after completing banking activities
- Keep operating systems and browsers updated

OTP SECURITY

OTP should never be shared with another person.

Vishal Pandey Bank employees will not ask customers to disclose OTPs over unsolicited calls.

FORGOT PASSWORD

If a customer forgets the Net Banking password, they should use the official password-reset process.

The customer may be required to complete identity verification.

ACCOUNT LOCKOUT

Multiple unsuccessful login attempts may temporarily lock the account as a security measure.

Customers should use the official account-unlock or password-reset process.

SUSPICIOUS ACTIVITY

If a customer suspects unauthorized access:

1. Change the Net Banking password immediately.
2. Block compromised cards if necessary.
3. Review recent transactions.
4. Contact Vishal Pandey Bank through an official support channel.
5. Report unauthorized transactions promptly.

IMPORTANT SECURITY RULE

Never provide your:
- Password
- OTP
- UPI PIN
- Debit card PIN
- CVV

to anyone claiming to be a bank representative.
