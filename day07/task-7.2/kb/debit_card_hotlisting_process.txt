VISHAL PANDEY BANK
Debit Card Hotlisting and Blocking Process

Document Type: Security Procedure
Bank: Vishal Pandey Bank
Version: 1.0
Effective Date: 2026-01-01

PURPOSE

Hotlisting is the process of permanently or temporarily blocking a debit card to prevent unauthorized transactions.

Customers should immediately hotlist their debit card if it is:
- Lost
- Stolen
- Suspected to be compromised
- Used for an unauthorized transaction
- Retained by an ATM

IMMEDIATE ACTION

If a customer discovers that a debit card is lost or stolen, the card should be blocked immediately.

AVAILABLE HOTLISTING CHANNELS

Customers may use supported Vishal Pandey Bank channels:

1. Mobile Banking
2. Net Banking
3. Customer Support
4. Authorized Branch
5. Other officially supported banking channels

MOBILE BANKING PROCESS

Typical process:

Step 1:
Log in to Vishal Pandey Bank Mobile Banking.

Step 2:
Open the Cards section.

Step 3:
Select Debit Card.

Step 4:
Select the affected card.

Step 5:
Choose Block or Hotlist Card.

Step 6:
Select the reason for blocking.

Step 7:
Confirm the request using the required authentication method.

Step 8:
Save the confirmation/reference number.

NET BANKING PROCESS

Step 1:
Log in to Vishal Pandey Bank Net Banking.

Step 2:
Navigate to Cards or Debit Card Services.

Step 3:
Select the debit card.

Step 4:
Choose Block/Hotlist.

Step 5:
Confirm the request.

Step 6:
Record the reference number displayed after successful submission.

CUSTOMER SUPPORT

Customers can contact the official Vishal Pandey Bank customer-support channel and request immediate card blocking.

The customer may be asked to provide information required to verify their identity.

BRANCH PROCESS

Customers may visit an authorized Vishal Pandey Bank branch and request debit-card blocking.

Identity verification may be required.

UNAUTHORIZED TRANSACTION

If a customer notices an unauthorized transaction:

1. Block the debit card immediately.
2. Report the transaction through an official Vishal Pandey Bank channel.
3. Record the complaint/reference number.
4. Provide supporting information if requested.
5. Monitor the account for additional unauthorized transactions.

CARD REPLACEMENT

After hotlisting, customers may request a replacement debit card.

Replacement fees, delivery timelines, and eligibility depend on the applicable card terms.

IMPORTANT SECURITY RULES

Customers must never share:
- OTP
- PIN
- CVV
- ATM PIN
- Internet banking password
- Mobile banking password

Do not wait to investigate the loss before blocking the card.
