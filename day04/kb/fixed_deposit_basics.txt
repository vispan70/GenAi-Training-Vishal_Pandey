Vishal Pandey Bank provides fictional Fixed Deposit terms from 6 months to 5 years.
The minimum amount required to open a Fixed Deposit is ₹5,000.
Customers can choose the deposit period during the Fixed Deposit creation process.
The fictional interest rate for a 1-year Fixed Deposit is 6% per year.
The fictional interest rate for a 3-year Fixed Deposit is 6.5% per year.
The fictional interest rate for a 5-year Fixed Deposit is 7% per year.
Interest is calculated according to the selected deposit terms.
Premature closure may result in a lower applicable interest rate.
Customers receive the maturity amount in their linked Savings Account.
The bank provides a fictional Fixed Deposit reference number after creation.
