Vishal Pandey Bank allows customers to raise a grievance through customer support.
The first level is Customer Support, which handles normal service complaints.
Customer Support has a fictional resolution target of 2 business days.
If unresolved, the complaint can be escalated to the Branch Service Manager.
The Branch Service Manager has a fictional resolution target of 3 business days.
If the issue remains unresolved, it can be escalated to the Grievance Officer.
The Grievance Officer has a fictional resolution target of 5 business days.
Customers should provide the complaint reference number during escalation.
Each escalation should include a brief description of the unresolved issue.
Customers can use the final escalation level when the previous level does not resolve the complaint.
