Vishal Pandey Bank offers Savings Accounts for personal banking customers.
The minimum opening balance for a standard Savings Account is ₹1,000.
Customers can open the account using the bank's fictional digital or branch process.
A customer must complete KYC before the account becomes fully active.
The fictional savings interest rate is 4% per year.
Interest is calculated on the daily closing balance and credited quarterly.
Customers receive a debit card after successful account activation.
Internet Banking and UPI can be activated after the account is active.
The standard monthly account maintenance charge is ₹50 if the balance falls below ₹1,000.
Customers can contact customer support for account-related questions or complaints.
