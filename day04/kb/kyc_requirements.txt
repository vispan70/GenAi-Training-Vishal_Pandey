Vishal Pandey Bank requires customers to complete KYC before full account activation.
Customers must provide one valid identity document for KYC verification.
Customers must also provide one valid address document when required.
The customer's name and date of birth must match the submitted information.
A registered mobile number is required for customer verification.
The bank may request additional documents if submitted information is unclear.
KYC information is reviewed according to the bank's fictional verification process.
Customers should keep their submitted documents valid and up to date.
Incomplete KYC can restrict some account services until verification is completed.
Customers can contact bank support to check the status of a KYC request.
