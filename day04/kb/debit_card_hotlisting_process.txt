Vishal Pandey Bank customers should hotlist a debit card immediately if it is lost or stolen.
A customer can request hotlisting through Internet Banking or the bank's support number.
The customer must provide the registered mobile number for verification.
A one-time verification code is sent to the registered mobile number.
After successful verification, the debit card is blocked immediately.
A hotlisted card cannot be used for ATM withdrawals or card purchases.
The bank records the hotlisting request with a unique service reference number.
Customers should keep the reference number for future communication.
A replacement debit card can be requested after the original card is hotlisted.
The fictional replacement card fee is ₹150.
