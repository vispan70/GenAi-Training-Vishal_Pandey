Vishal Pandey Bank customers can activate Internet Banking after their account is active.
The customer must have a registered mobile number with the bank.
Activation requires the customer's account number and customer identification number.
A verification code is sent to the registered mobile number during activation.
The customer must create a fictional Internet Banking username and password.
The password must be changed if the customer suspects unauthorized access.
Internet Banking can be used to view balances and transaction history.
Customers can also use Internet Banking to request debit card hotlisting.
The customer should never share passwords or verification codes with another person.
Customers can contact support if Internet Banking activation fails repeatedly.
