Vishal Pandey Bank supports UPI transactions for eligible Savings Account customers.
The fictional daily UPI transaction limit is ₹1,00,000 per customer.
The maximum fictional UPI transaction count is 20 transactions per day.
A UPI transaction may fail because of insufficient account balance.
Transactions may also fail because the daily UPI limit has been reached.
A temporary technical issue can also cause a UPI transaction to fail.
Customers should first check their balance and transaction history after a failure.
If money is debited but the transaction fails, the customer should wait for the status to update.
The bank's fictional normal reversal period for a failed debit is 2 business days.
Customers can raise a complaint if the amount is not reversed after 2 business days.
